# Work Ledger — promises made

> The tracker tracks tickets. This tracks promises. An entry is anything you told a person you would do — these build or burn trust, and no tool captures them.

| Promised | To | Due | Ticket ref | Status |
|----------|----|----|-----------|--------|
| <what you committed to> | <who> | YYYY-MM-DD | ABC-123 | open / done / renegotiated |

## Rules

- Add the entry in the same hour the promise is made.
- "Renegotiated" is a status, not a failure — but it requires having told the person.
- Review every open entry during Monday planning.
